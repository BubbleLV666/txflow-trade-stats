Trading stats for any address on TxFlow

Paste an address — the site queries the public TxFlow API directly and shows equity, PnL, volume, fees, positions with liquidation prices, maker/taker split, per-market breakdown, funding, deposits and withdrawals. No keys or sign-in needed; the address is sent nowhere except the TxFlow API.

TxFlow points are not shown per address: the API returns them only to the wallet owner after sign-in. There is also no per-address leaderboard rank or equity history in the public API.

Ranking of the known TxFlow wallets by trading volume. Volume, PnL and balance of each wallet are calculated by TxFlow; the data is refreshed continuously (24 hours every 15 minutes, 7 days every hour, 30 days every 6 hours, all time daily).

Rankings of the past trading competitions of TxFlow (Trader Royale, Bull Pit): each has its own period and prize pool. TxFlow publishes no all-time or per-period ranking of PnL or volume, only these top 50 lists.

Unofficial project, not affiliated with TxFlow. Data comes from the public API api.txflow.com; it may lag or contain exchange errors.

This site connects to the TxFlow API

This site reads data directly from the public TxFlow API, from your browser and your IP address.

If you open many wallets in a short time or refresh the page very often, the request limit can be exceeded and TxFlow may temporarily block your access to app.txflow.com: a “too many requests” message then appears on the whole page. The block usually goes away after a few minutes.

This is an unofficial site, not affiliated with TxFlow.
